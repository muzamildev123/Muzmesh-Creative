Homeowners compare three or four remodelers before they call one. They look at your photos, check you are real, and send an enquiry to more than one company. The first one to reply well usually gets the job.

## What we set up

- **A portfolio website** that shows your best projects by type: kitchens, bathrooms, additions.
- **Quote request forms** that ask the right questions up front: project type, budget, timeline, location.
- **Instant replies** so every homeowner hears back in minutes, even when you are on site.
- **A simple CRM** so every lead is tracked from first enquiry to signed job.
- **Follow-up emails** on day 3 and day 7 for leads that went quiet.
- **Monthly social posts** from your finished projects.
