Version 1.0 (draft) · 30 Oct 2026 · Owner: Muzamil Awan

Finished, section-by-section copy for the new homepage (`/`). It expands [positioning.md, section 6](positioning.md#6-homepage-copy) and follows the offer and referral rules in sections 3 and 4 of that doc. If this file and positioning.md disagree on price, limits or rules, positioning.md wins and this file gets fixed.

**How to read this file**

- Text in **bold labels** (Eyebrow, Heading, Body, Button) is the copy that goes on the page, word for word.
- Lines starting with `Note:` are for the designer and developer. They never go on the page.
- `[PLACEHOLDER]` means real content is missing. `[CONFIRM]` means a decision is pending. Neither can go live as-is. We never publish invented results, client names, testimonials or statistics.

**Voice:** plain, confident, a little dry. Short sentences. No "revolutionize", "supercharge", "unlock", "seamless", "cutting-edge" or "in today's fast-paced world". We sound like the people doing the work, not a sales deck. "Ready for the AI era" is the theme, but we show it with specifics (AI search, structured content) instead of saying "AI-powered".

## 4. Problem

**Eyebrow:** The problem

**Heading:** You built the product. The launch still looks like a template.

**Body:**
Most AI startup sites look the same: purple gradients, floating 3D shapes, and copy written by the model you're competing with. Investors, users and early hires have seen it a hundred times, and they scroll past.

Behind the page it's worse. Your socials went quiet after launch week. Nobody knows where sign-ups come from. Waitlist emails sit in a spreadsheet. And when someone asks ChatGPT about your category, you're not in the answer.

None of this is hard. It's just not your job, and you don't have anyone whose job it is.

Note: three short paragraphs, no list. If the design wants a visual, a side-by-side of "generic AI startup hero" vs "a page that looks like a company" works, as long as the "generic" side is a mock we made, not a real company's site.

## 12. FAQ

**Heading:** Questions founders ask us

Note: every question and answer here goes into `FAQPage` schema on the page. Keep the answers as plain text in the CMS so the schema and the page always match.

**What does "ready for the AI era" actually mean?**
Two things. Your site is set up to be found by AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI answers) with schema, an FAQ, `llms.txt` and clean structure. And your content lives in a headless CMS with an API, so you can plug in chatbots, search or agents later without rebuilding the site.

**Is the design really custom, or is it AI-generated?**
Every page is designed by a person in Figma, on our own design system. We don't use AI site generators or stock templates. That's how we keep it to $500 without it looking like everyone else's.

**How can you do all this for $500?**
We've built our own design system, Sanity starter and tracking and CRM setup, and we reuse them on every project. You get a page designed for your startup, without paying for us to reinvent the plumbing each time. If you need more pages or a fully custom build, we'll quote it separately.

**What is AEO or GEO?**
Answer engine optimization and generative engine optimization. It's SEO for AI answers: making it easy for tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity to understand what you do and mention you when people ask about your category. Nobody can guarantee an AI will cite you, but we set up everything that makes it more likely.

**Who owns the site and the accounts?**
You do. Hosting, domain, CMS, CRM and analytics are on your own accounts. If you ever leave, everything stays with you and keeps working.

**What do you need from us?**
Your logo, product screenshots, a rough draft of your copy and access to your accounts. We'll shape and edit the copy, but we don't write it from scratch.

**Which tools do you use?**
Sanity for the CMS, `[CONFIRM: Astro or Next.js]` for the site, `[CONFIRM: GA4 or PostHog]` for analytics and `[CONFIRM: HubSpot]` for the CRM. If you already use something else, tell us on the call.

**What's in the $200 a month?**
Site edits and updates, up to `[CONFIRM: 8]` social posts a month on your 2 platforms, support with a reply within 2 business days, and a one-page monthly report. Unused hours and posts don't roll over.

**Can we cancel?**
`[CONFIRM: After the first 3 months]`, any time with 30 days' notice. Your site and accounts keep working because they're yours.

**How does the referral discount work?**
Refer another AI startup while you're on the retainer. Once they pay their $500 setup, you get 40% off your next monthly bill. One discount per bill, and extra referrals carry over.

**What if we need more pages, an app or a full brand?**
We'll quote it separately. The $500 setup is one landing page, done well.
