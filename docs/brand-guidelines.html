01 · Direction

We picked The Mesh.

All three drop the old electric blue, which sits in the purple-gradient look the new copy argues against. Each one says "a person made this" in a different way.

A · The MeshChosen

An M drawn as one line through a 3 × 3 grid of points: the mesh, connected by hand. The yellow node is where the founder's product meets the market. Lowercase wordmark in Archivo Expanded.

Ink, paper, marker yellow. Archivo Expanded, Archivo, JetBrains Mono.

B · Margin note

A designer's red-pen circle around "Mesh", like feedback on a print-out. Warmer and more playful. The circle is redrawn by hand for each use, so it never looks generated.

Ballpoint navy, paper white, correction red. Bricolage Grotesque, Archivo.

C · Frame

The name inside Figma-style frame corners with a layer label. It says "designed in Figma" literally. Most technical of the three, closest to how dev-tool startups look.

Graphite, bone, frame teal. JetBrains Mono, Archivo.

Why A won: it is the only one with a symbol that works alone at 16px, it turns the company name into a picture, and yellow-on-ink stands out in a LinkedIn feed full of purple and blue.

02 · Brand story and positioning

Designed by people. Found by AI.

MuzMesh Creative gets AI startups launched online: a landing page that doesn't look AI-generated, social media, AEO, tracking and CRM. $500 to set up, $200 a month to keep it running.

Most AI startup sites look the same: purple gradients, floating 3D shapes, and copy written by the model they're competing with. Founders building real products end up looking like a template. We're three people in Pakistan who design every page by hand in Figma, build it on a headless CMS, and set it up so ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI answers can find it. Then we keep it running, so the founders can stay on the product.

The brand has to prove the "not AI slop" point on sight. That's why it is plain, typographic and a little blunt: heavy expanded type, ink on paper, and one hand-marked highlight. No gradients, no glow, no robots.

Pillar

Found by AI

Schema, FAQ, llms.txt and clean structure, so AI answers can cite the startup.

Pillar

Built for AI

Content in a headless CMS with an API, ready for chatbots, search and agents later.

Pillar

Not made by AI

Designed by people in Figma on our own system. It doesn't look like everyone else's.

Who we talk to

Founders of AI startups, pre-launch to seed, 1 to 15 people. Technical or product-minded, no designer or marketer yet. They need to look credible to users, investors and early hires, fast.

Names, spelled right
  • MuzMesh Creative in full the first time, MuzMesh after. One word, two capital Ms.
  • AI Starter Kit, always with those capitals. Never "starter pack".
  • The lowercase muzmesh is the logo only. Never type it that way in running text.
  • Prices as $500 and $200 a month (or $200 / month on price cards). USD, no ranges.
03 · Voice and tone

Plain, confident, a little dry.

We sound like the people doing the work, not a sales deck. Short sentences. Specifics over adjectives. Prices out loud.

Plain

Say what it is. "A landing page, social, AI search, tracking and CRM", not "a holistic digital presence".

Specific

Show the AI era with real things: schema, llms.txt, an API. Never just "AI-powered".

Honest

No invented results, logos, counters or testimonials. If we only have one case study, we show one.

Don't

Supercharge your AI startup with cutting-edge, AI-powered web solutions.

Do

Launch your AI startup like it's already a real company.

Don't

We leverage AI to deliver seamless, next-gen experiences.

Do

Don't

Contact us to discuss a custom package for your budget.

$500 to set up. $200 a month. No calls to "discuss budget".

Trusted by 50+ innovative startups worldwide.

Unverified number. Never publish it.

04 · Logo

One line through the mesh.

The symbol is an M drawn as a single stroke through a 3 × 3 grid. Two unused points stay visible, so you can see it was plotted, not generated. The yellow centre node is the only colour in the mark.

Primary lockup · muzmesh-logo.svg
Symbol · favicon, small spaces
Avatar · social profiles

Construction

3 × 3 grid, 32-unit steps. Stroke is 13 units with round joins. Centre node 11 units, ringed in ink.

Clear space and size

Clear space on every side: one grid step, a third of the symbol's height.

Lockup at least 110px wide. Symbol at least 16px. Below 24px, drop the two loose dots.

Colour

Ink symbol and wordmark on paper, paper on ink. The centre node is always marker yellow with an ink ring, on any background.

Write the name in text as MuzMesh Creative. The lowercase "muzmesh" is the logo only.

05 · Colour

Ink, paper, one marker.

Rules

Layout
  • Content max width 1200px. Prose max 640px (about 65 characters).
  • 12-column grid, 24px gutters, 16px side margin on mobile.
  • Radius: 4px buttons and inputs, 8px cards and screenshots, 14px monogram.

  • No drop shadows, no blur, no glassmorphism.
  • Motion: 150 to 250ms, ease cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). Respect reduced motion.
08 · Imagery

Show the work, not the vibe.

Every image should prove a person made something real. If an image could appear on any AI startup's site, it doesn't go on ours.

Real screenshotsSites we built, in a plain browser frame, with client permission.

Real peopleThe three of us, at real desks, in natural light. No stock teams.
Do
  • Product and site screenshots at real size, cropped tight.
  • Before and after pairs (with permission).
  • Hand annotations in marker yellow over screenshots.
  • Simple line icons, 1.5px stroke, ink only.
Don't
  • Purple or blue gradients, glowing orbs, 3D blobs.
  • AI-generated people, robots, brains, neural-network nodes.
  • Fake dashboards with invented numbers.
  • Client logos without written permission.

Rules
  • 12 words or fewer on the image. Detail goes in the caption.
  • Logo bottom-left, small. Handle or URL bottom-right.
  • Captions follow the voice rules: no emoji walls, no hype words, no "excited to announce".
  • One CTA per post at most: "Plan my launch" or a link.

10 · Tailwind theme

Tokens for the web/ build.
