The founder is the hero. We're the guide.

Our story for the AI Starter Kit, laid out with the StoryBrand (SB7) framework. Every headline on the site should be about what the founder wants, not about what we do.

The one-liner
Most AI startups now look and sound the same, because their brand came from one prompt. The AI Starter Kit gives you a launch designed by people in about a week, so you stand out and people remember you.

Short version: We help AI startups stop looking like everyone else: a launch designed by people for $500, kept running for $200 a month.

01
The hero

An AI founder who wants to stand out.

Who they are

Founders of an AI startup, pre-launch to seed, 1-15 people. Usually technical or product people. No designer, marketer or web developer on the team.

What they want

To stand out and be remembered in a crowded AI market, so users, investors and early hires pick them and not the lookalike next door.

02
The problem · the villain

AI sameness: brands made from one prompt.

Same purple gradients, same floating shapes, same generated logo, copy written by the model they compete with. When every startup comes from the same prompt, nobody can tell them apart.

External

No difference between brands. Socials went quiet after launch week. Nobody knows where sign-ups come from. ChatGPT has never heard of them.

"Your AI startup shouldn't look like it came from the same prompt as everyone else's."

Internal · this one sells

They feel invisible. Proud of the product, a little embarrassed to send the link, and stretched too thin to fix it.

"You built something different. Your brand should show it."

Philosophical

A good product shouldn't be ignored because its brand looks like everyone else's.

"Good products deserve to be noticed."

03
The guide

MuzMesh: we get it, and we can help.

Empathy

"We know what it's like to build something good and watch it get ignored."

Needs confirming. A real story from the team beats a written line.

Authority we can show today
  • Topklik.ai: automated lead system for an AI startup (result line still pending)
  • Our own system: design system, Sanity starter and CRM setup, reused every time
  • Published price: $500 + $200/month, no "contact us"
  • Three people: you talk to the ones doing the work
04
The plan

Three steps. Live in about a week.

  1. Book a 15-minute call

    Tell us what you're building and when you launch.

  2. Get your plan the same day

    A one-page proposal: what we build, when, and what we need from you.

  3. Launch, then we keep it running

    Site, social, AI search, tracking and CRM. Then $200 a month keeps it all moving.

06–07
What's at stake

Lost in the crowd, or the one people remember.

Failure
  • You launch to silence. People scroll past a page they've seen a hundred times.
  • Someone asks ChatGPT about your category and it names a competitor.
  • Sign-ups slip through a spreadsheet and never hear back.
  • You lose weeks on the website instead of the product.
Success
  • Launch day looks like a real company launched.
  • You show up when people ask AI tools about your category.
  • Every sign-up is tracked, answered and in one place.
  • Socials keep posting, and you're back on the product.
The identity change
Another AI startup→The one people remember
Gap to close

"Unique" needs a brand step.

Today the kit excludes brand identity, so standing out is a promise we only half keep. Proposal: add a light brand direction step to the $500 setup. A 30-minute session, then one page with the positioning line, colours, type, voice, and one visual idea no competitor uses. A full logo or identity stays a separate quote.
