# Hero visual v4: what the references teach us, plus 4 new prompts

## Review of the 4 references

| Reference | Idea | Composition | Visual | Keep / avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **"Make your move."** (man on blocks) | Weak, generic "take a step" | Strong: the man walks *through* the headline, so text and image become one layer | Glowing edges, blue fog, dark gradient. This is the exact "AI look" we ban | **Keep** the text-and-object layering. **Avoid** the glow, fog and blue. |
| **Cloche reveal** (black hands, yellow) | Clear: "something is being revealed" = a launch | Best of the four. Vertical, centred, huge empty space, the gap between lid and plate makes you wait | Two colours only, and they are almost exactly ours (#FFD60A + #0E0E10). Matte, no shine | **Keep** almost everything. It already looks like MuzMesh. |
| **"Ctrl"** (glass key) | Smart: the headline word *is* the object, a simple pun | One object, overlapping a huge word, highlighter box on "Relaxa," (like our marker bar) | Neon lime glass glow is trendy and will date fast | **Keep** the pun and the word/object overlap. **Avoid** glass and glow. |
| **"Step Out" (esc + ladder)** | Smartest story: a tiny ladder climbing out of the Esc key = escape the ordinary | Tight crop on a real keyboard gives scale; object breaks the grid | Two colours, matte, realistic. Logo and small text feel crowded | **Keep** "break the grid of a familiar object" and the matte realism. |

**What the good ones have in common**
1. **One familiar object, one twist.** Not a scene, one thing.
2. **The headline and the picture are the same idea.** You "get it" in one second.
3. **Two colours.** For us that is yellow #FFD60A and ink #0E0E10, with paper #F2F2EE.
4. **Object overlaps the big headline**, which creates depth.
5. **Huge empty space** and matte, real-looking materials. No glow.

**How to build it on the website:** ask ChatGPT for the object on a plain background, cut it out (transparent PNG), and layer it over the real headline text in code. You get the "Ctrl" overlap effect with sharp text that Google and AI search can still read. Each prompt below has a **mockup** version (with headline, to judge the idea) and a **cutout** version (object only, for the site).

## Prompt 1 (my pick): Ctrl + V
**Idea:** the villain is copy-paste. A brand made from one prompt is a Ctrl+V. The keys are worn out from overuse. One key is hand-made.
**Headline:** *Your brand isn't a Ctrl+V.*

**Mockup**
```
A minimal, realistic advertising poster, portrait 4:5, 1600 x 2000 px.

Background: plain warm off-white paper, exact colour #F2F2EE, with a very soft natural shadow at the bottom. Lots of empty space.

Top half: a huge bold headline in a wide, heavy, expanded sans-serif (like Archivo Expanded ExtraBold), colour #0E0E10, very tight letter spacing and tight line height, centred, on two lines:
"Your brand isn't
a Ctrl+V."
Behind the word "Ctrl+V." sits one thick lemon-yellow (#FFD60A) highlighter-style rectangle, slightly rough at the edges like a real marker.

Bottom half: a close, realistic macro photo of three matte black keyboard keys in a row, "Ctrl", "C" and "V". The C and V keys are worn shiny and smooth from overuse, their letters half rubbed off, with tiny scratches. The three keys slightly overlap the bottom of the headline, so the text sits behind them.

Next to them, one extra key has been pulled out of the row and stands on its own: hand-carved from wood and painted matte lemon yellow (#FFD60A), slightly imperfect, blank, with a faint pencil line on top. A short black pencil lies next to it.

Lighting: soft daylight from the top left, real contact shadows, matte materials.
Camera: 100mm macro, f/5.6, subtle film grain.
Only colours: #F2F2EE, #0E0E10, greys, #FFD60A.
Do NOT add: glow, glass, neon, gradients, purple, blue, logos, extra text, watermark.
```

**Cutout (for the site)**
```
Realistic macro product photo, square 2000 x 2000 px, isolated on a plain pure white background with only a soft contact shadow: three matte black keyboard keys "Ctrl", "C", "V" in a row, the C and V worn shiny with half-rubbed-off letters, plus one separate hand-carved wooden key painted matte lemon yellow (#FFD60A), blank, with a faint pencil line, and a short black pencil beside it. Soft daylight from top left, 100mm macro, f/5.6. No glow, no glass, no other text, no logos.
```

## Prompt 2: The reveal (closest to the cloche)
**Idea:** a launch is a reveal. Under the lid is not a generic logo but a hand-drawn brand sketch.
**Headline:** *Served in about a week.* (or *Your launch, hand-made.*)

**Mockup**
```
A minimal, surreal, realistic poster, portrait 2:3, 1600 x 2400 px.

Background: flat solid lemon yellow, exact colour #FFD60A, no texture, no gradient.

A matte black (#0E0E10) sculpted hand comes in from the top right and lifts a matte black serving cloche by its knob, holding it high in the air. A second matte black hand comes in from the bottom left and holds a matte black serving tray. There is a big gap between the cloche and the tray.

On the tray sits one thing only: a small, crisp sheet of warm off-white paper (#F2F2EE) with a bold hand-drawn logo sketch in black ink and pencil construction lines around it, clearly drawn by a person. A tiny black pencil lies beside it.

In the empty yellow space between the cloche and the tray, a bold headline in a wide, heavy, expanded sans-serif (Archivo Expanded ExtraBold), colour #0E0E10, tight letter spacing, centred:
"Served in
about a week."

Matte, soft studio light, very soft shadows, huge empty space, perfectly balanced vertical composition.
Only colours: #FFD60A, #0E0E10, #F2F2EE.
Do NOT add: shine, glow, gradients, extra text, logos, watermark.
```

**Cutout**
Same prompt, but remove the headline and say: "background flat solid #FFD60A, no text". Use the yellow as a full-width hero band with the headline in code.

## Prompt 3: The Enter key
**Idea:** most AI brands came from one press of Enter. Show the key, cracked from being pressed by everyone, and a pencil resting on it.
**Headline:** *Most brands are one Enter key away from looking the same.*

**Mockup**
```
A minimal, realistic advertising poster, portrait 4:5, 1600 x 2000 px.

Background: plain warm off-white #F2F2EE, huge empty space.

Centre: one oversized matte black (#0E0E10) "Enter" keyboard key, photographed close up at a slight angle. It is pressed down, worn smooth and shiny in the middle, with a hairline crack across it, as if a million people pressed it. A sharpened lemon-yellow (#FFD60A) pencil rests diagonally across the top of the key, breaking its shape, with fresh pencil shavings beside it.

Above the key, a big bold headline in a wide, heavy, expanded sans-serif (Archivo Expanded ExtraBold), colour #0E0E10, tight letter spacing, left aligned:
"One prompt.
Same brand."
The top of the Enter key overlaps the bottom of the word "brand." slightly.

Soft daylight from the left, real contact shadows, matte materials, 100mm macro, f/5.6, subtle film grain.
Only colours: #F2F2EE, #0E0E10, greys, #FFD60A.
Do NOT add: glow, glass, neon, gradients, purple, blue, logos, extra text, watermark.
```

## Prompt 4: Escape the template (closest to the esc ladder)
**Idea:** a website template is a grid. A tiny designer climbs out of it with a yellow pencil.
**Headline:** *Step out of the template.*

**Mockup**
```
A minimal, realistic, slightly playful advertising poster, portrait 4:5, 1600 x 2000 px.

Background: plain warm off-white #F2F2EE, empty top half.

Top: a big bold headline in a wide, heavy, expanded sans-serif (Archivo Expanded ExtraBold), colour #0E0E10, tight letter spacing:
"Step out of
the template."

Bottom half: a close, tightly cropped macro photo of a grid of identical matte black square tiles, like a keyboard or a template grid, filling the bottom of the frame at an angle. One tile is lifted and tipped open like a hatch. From the hole underneath, a tiny lemon-yellow (#FFD60A) wooden ladder leans out, and a real yellow pencil (#FFD60A) pokes up out of the hole like it is climbing out. The inside of the hole is painted lemon yellow.

Soft daylight from the top right, real contact shadows, matte rubber and wood textures, 60mm macro, f/4, subtle film grain.
Only colours: #F2F2EE, #0E0E10, greys, #FFD60A.
Do NOT add: glow, gradients, purple, blue, logos, extra text, watermark.
```

## My ranking
1. **Ctrl + V.** It's the clearest attack on the villain ("one prompt = copy-paste") and the clearest pun, and it sits on your light page.
2. **The reveal.** It's the most beautiful, and on-brand from the start, but it says "launch" more than "unique".
3. **Enter key.** Strong, but its headline is darker in tone.
4. **Step out of the template.** It's fun, but it's the closest to the reference, so it could look borrowed.
