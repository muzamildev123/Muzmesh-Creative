# Hero visual: ChatGPT image prompts

**Story the picture tells:** most AI startups look the same because their brand came from one prompt. One launch, designed by people, stands out and gets remembered.

**How to use:** paste one prompt into ChatGPT (image generation). Prompt A is the one I'd start with. It makes the visual for the right side of the hero; the headline and buttons stay as real text on the page, which keeps them sharp and readable by Google and AI search.

**Tips**
- Image tools often get small text wrong. If a word comes out broken, reply: "Keep everything the same, but fix the text to read exactly: ..."
- If it adds purple, glow or 3D, reply: "Remove all gradients, glow and 3D. Flat, print-like, only the colours I listed."
- Ask for a PNG at 1600 x 1600 (square) or 1600 x 2000 (4:5) for the hero's right column.

## Prompt A (recommended): "One of these is not like the others"

```
Create a flat, editorial, print-style illustration for a website hero section. Square format, 1600 x 1600 px.

THE IDEA
A neat grid of 12 small website cards, 4 columns x 3 rows, seen straight on (no perspective, no tilt). 11 of them are the same generic "AI startup" landing page, copied over and over. One card, in the second row, third column, is completely different: hand-designed, bold, confident. It is the only thing your eye goes to.

BACKGROUND
Solid flat colour #F2F2EE (warm off-white paper). No texture, no gradient, no vignette.

THE 11 "SAME" CARDS
- Every one is identical: white #FFFFFF card, 1px border #D9D9DF, corner radius 8px.
- Inside each: a small fuzzy blob shape in pale grey #D9D9DF where a "glowing orb" would be, a grey headline bar, two thin grey text lines, and a grey rounded "Get started" button.
- All in light greys only (#D9D9DF and #A1A1AA). Washed-out, slightly faded, forgettable. Same layout, same spacing, like they came off one stamp.
- Above the grid, very small, in a monospace font (JetBrains Mono style), uppercase, wide letter spacing, colour #55555F: "GENERATED FROM ONE PROMPT"

THE ONE STANDOUT CARD
- Same size as the others, but lifted slightly with a crisp hard offset shadow (8px down and right, solid #0E0E10, no blur).
- Background near-black #0E0E10, 2px border #0E0E10, radius 8px.
- A short, bold headline in a wide, heavy, expanded sans-serif (like Archivo Expanded ExtraBold), white #F2F2EE, tight letter spacing. The headline reads: "Yours."
- One thick yellow highlighter-style bar #FFD60A sitting under the word, like a marker stroke drawn by hand.
- A small yellow #FFD60A rectangular button with square-ish corners (4px) and black #0E0E10 text: "Launch".
- Small details that feel hand-made: a tiny pencil tick mark, a ruler measurement line, a small crosshair, drawn in thin #A1A1AA lines around the card edge, like a designer's working marks in Figma.
- Below this card, small monospace uppercase label in #0E0E10 on a yellow #FFD60A tag: "DESIGNED BY PEOPLE"

STYLE RULES
- Flat vector look, sharp edges, like a Swiss poster or a clean product diagram.
- Only these colours: #F2F2EE, #FFFFFF, #D9D9DF, #A1A1AA, #55555F, #0E0E10, #FFD60A. Nothing else.
- No purple, no blue, no gradients, no glow, no glowing orbs, no 3D shapes, no robots, no brains, no circuit lines, no people, no stock photo look.
- Lots of empty space and even spacing. Calm grid, one loud card.
- The only text in the image is: "GENERATED FROM ONE PROMPT", "Yours.", "Launch", "DESIGNED BY PEOPLE". Spell them exactly.
```

## Prompt B: full hero mockup (to see the whole section, not the final image)

Use this to get a quick picture of the full hero for discussion. Build the real one in Figma or code.

```
Design a desktop website hero section mockup, 1440 x 900 px, flat and clean, like a real Figma design shot straight on.

BRAND
- Page background #F2F2EE. Main text and dark shapes #0E0E10. Secondary text #55555F. Borders #D9D9DF. Accent yellow #FFD60A (used only as a fill, never as text colour on the light background).
- Headline font: wide, heavy expanded sans-serif (Archivo Expanded ExtraBold), very tight letter spacing and tight line height.
- Body font: Archivo Regular. Small labels: JetBrains Mono, uppercase, wide letter spacing.

TOP BAR (height about 80px)
- Left: wordmark "MuzMesh" in the heavy expanded font, #0E0E10.
- Middle: small links "What's included · Pricing · Work · Referral · Blog" in #55555F.
- Right: black #0E0E10 button with light text "Plan my launch", radius 8px.

LEFT HALF (text, vertically centred)
- Small label: "THE AI STARTER KIT · FOR AI STARTUPS", JetBrains Mono, 12px, uppercase, #55555F.
- Headline over two lines, very large (about 96px), #0E0E10: "Get AI-ready in about a week." with a thick yellow #FFD60A marker bar behind the words "about a week", like a highlighter stroke.
- Body text, 20px, #55555F, max 3 lines: "A hand-designed landing page that's ready for the AI era: found in ChatGPT and Google AI answers, tracked from day one, and connected to your CRM."
- Two buttons in a row: a yellow #FFD60A button with black text "Plan my launch in 15 minutes", radius 8px; and an outline button with 2px #0E0E10 border, black text "See what's included".
- Small print under the buttons, 14px, #55555F: "About a week from when we have your content."

RIGHT HALF (the visual)
- A grid of 9 identical, pale grey, faded generic startup website cards (white #FFFFFF, 1px #D9D9DF border, grey blob, grey bars, grey "Get started" button).
- The centre card is different: black #0E0E10, bold white headline "Yours.", a yellow #FFD60A highlight bar, a hard black offset shadow, thin grey designer measurement marks around it. A small yellow tag under it reads "DESIGNED BY PEOPLE".
- A small mono label above the grid in #55555F: "GENERATED FROM ONE PROMPT".

RULES
- Lots of white space, strong alignment on a 12-column grid.
- No purple, no gradients, no glow, no orbs, no 3D, no robots, no people, no photos.
- Spell every word exactly as written.
```

## Prompt C: "Same prompt, same face"

A bolder, more poster-like take. Good for social posts and the launch announcement too.

```
Create a flat editorial poster-style illustration, 1600 x 1600 px, background solid #F2F2EE.

A long horizontal row of 7 identical masks or faces drawn as simple flat grey outlines (#A1A1AA lines on #FFFFFF shapes), lined up in a perfect row, all the same, expressionless. Above them, in tiny monospace uppercase letters (JetBrains Mono style), #55555F: "PROMPT: MAKE ME A STARTUP BRAND".

The 5th one in the row is different: solid near-black #0E0E10, with a bold, unique, hand-drawn character, a confident shape and one bright yellow #FFD60A detail (a stripe or a marker stroke across it). It steps half a step forward out of the line and has a hard flat black offset shadow, no blur.

Under the row, large, wide, heavy expanded sans-serif text (Archivo Expanded ExtraBold), #0E0E10, tight letter spacing: "Don't look like the prompt."

Rules: only the colours #F2F2EE, #FFFFFF, #A1A1AA, #55555F, #0E0E10, #FFD60A. Flat vector, sharp edges, lots of space. No purple, no gradients, no glow, no 3D, no robots, no realistic people. Spell the text exactly.
```

## Prompt D: "Before and after" split

Simple and very clear. Works well on mobile, where a 12-card grid gets too small.

```
Create a flat, clean, editorial illustration, 1600 x 2000 px (portrait 4:5), split into two halves top and bottom with a thin 1px #D9D9DF line between them.

TOP HALF, background #FFFFFF:
A stack of 5 generic AI startup landing pages, fanned slightly on top of each other, all identical: pale grey blob, grey headline bars, grey "Get started" buttons, all in #D9D9DF and #A1A1AA. Faded and forgettable. Small monospace uppercase label in the top left corner, #55555F: "ONE PROMPT".

BOTTOM HALF, background #0E0E10:
One single landing page card, sharp and bold, shown straight on. Black #17171D card with a 1px #2C2C35 border. A big wide heavy expanded headline in #F2F2EE (Archivo Expanded ExtraBold style) reading "Remembered." with a thick yellow #FFD60A marker stroke under it. A yellow #FFD60A button with black text "Launch". Thin designer measurement marks and a small crosshair in #A1A1AA around the card, like it was laid out by hand in Figma. Small monospace uppercase label in the top left corner, #A1A1AA: "DESIGNED BY PEOPLE".

Rules: only the colours #FFFFFF, #F2F2EE, #D9D9DF, #A1A1AA, #55555F, #0E0E10, #17171D, #2C2C35, #FFD60A. Flat vector, sharp, print-like. No purple, no gradients, no glow, no orbs, no 3D, no robots, no people. Spell the text exactly.
```

## Brand reference used

| Token | Hex | Use in the visual |
|---|---|---|
| paper | #F2F2EE | Main background |
| card | #FFFFFF | The "same" cards |
| line | #D9D9DF | Borders, grey blobs |
| mist | #A1A1AA | Faded text bars, designer marks |
| graphite | #55555F | Small labels on light background |
| ink | #0E0E10 | Standout card, headline, shadow |
| ink-2 / line-ink | #17171D / #2C2C35 | Card and border on dark |
| marker | #FFD60A | Accent fill only, never text on light background |

Fonts: Archivo Expanded ExtraBold (800) for headlines, tight spacing (-3%). Archivo for body. JetBrains Mono, uppercase, +8% spacing, for small labels.

Notes from the brief that these prompts follow: no purple gradients, glowing orbs or 3D shapes (landing-page-copy.md, hero note), and "in about a week" instead of "5 days" until it's proven on real clients.
