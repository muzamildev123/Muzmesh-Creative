# Hero visual v2: realistic photo prompts

The first round (flat cards) looked like AI slop. This round is real-looking studio photography, in the spirit of the "one orange chair" reference: a crowd of identical things, and one that stands out. The standout object is always MuzMesh marker yellow (#FFD60A) against near-black (#0E0E10), so the photo is on brand.

**How to use**
- Paste one prompt into ChatGPT. Start with Prompt 1.
- Keep text OUT of the photo. The headline and buttons live on the page in Archivo Expanded, not in the image.
- If it looks too shiny or plastic, reply: "Make it look like a real photo from a camera: natural imperfections, real dust and scuffs, softer reflections, film grain, less perfect."
- If the yellow drifts to orange, reply: "Make the yellow a clean lemon-yellow, exactly #FFD60A, not orange or gold."
- The hero is a light page (#F2F2EE). A dark photo works best as a framed image in the right column with 14px rounded corners, or as a full-width dark hero band (#0E0E10) with light text on top. Prompt 4 is the light-background option.

---

## Prompt 1 (recommended): one yellow chair

```
A realistic editorial studio photograph, shot from slightly above and in front, of a large room filled with rows of identical matte black moulded plastic chairs, packed tightly and stretching into the dark background. Every chair is exactly the same: same model, same matte black #0E0E10 finish, same angle.

In the middle of the frame, one chair is different: the same shape but in a clean, saturated lemon yellow (exact colour #FFD60A, not orange, not gold), with a satin finish. It is turned very slightly toward the camera, as if someone placed it by hand.

Lighting: one soft overhead spotlight falls on the yellow chair. The black chairs fade into deep shadow around it. Low-key, moody, high contrast, but with real detail in the blacks (no crushed pure black).

Camera: full-frame camera, 50mm lens, f/2.8, shallow depth of field so the chairs in the front row and far rows are softly out of focus. Natural film grain, real-world imperfections: tiny scuffs and dust on the chairs, slightly uneven floor.

Colours: only near-black (#0E0E10, #17171D), dark grey reflections, and the one yellow (#FFD60A). No other colours.

Format: portrait 4:5, 1600 x 2000 px. Leave clean dark space at the top for a headline.

Do NOT add: any text, logos, watermarks, people, glow effects, lens flares, purple or blue light, 3D render look, or a plastic CGI shine. It must look like a real photo from a professional photographer, not an AI image.
```

## Prompt 2: the one hand-made business card (closest to what MuzMesh sells)

```
A realistic top-down studio product photograph of a dark matte surface (#0E0E10, like black paper or slate) covered in a neat grid of identical plain grey business cards, all the same size, all printed with the same generic soft gradient blob logo, perfectly aligned. They look mass-produced and forgettable.

One card in the grid is different: a thick, premium, lemon-yellow card (exact colour #FFD60A) with clean, sharp letterpress edges, slightly raised off the surface and tilted a few degrees, casting a real soft shadow. Next to it lies a black pencil and a small steel ruler, as if a designer just finished it by hand.

Lighting: soft daylight from the top left, gentle real shadows, visible paper texture and fibres on every card.

Camera: 100mm macro lens, f/5.6, shot straight down, sharp across the grid with very slight falloff at the edges. Subtle film grain.

Colours: only near-black #0E0E10, mid greys, warm off-white #F2F2EE highlights, and the one yellow #FFD60A.

Format: square 1600 x 1600 px.

Do NOT add: any readable text, logos, watermarks, hands, people, glow, purple, gradients on the yellow card, or a CGI look. It must look like a real product photo.
```

## Prompt 3: the one finished product in a factory line

```
A realistic, cinematic photograph of a long industrial conveyor belt in a dim factory, carrying a single-file line of identical matte grey boxes, all the same shape and all stamped the same way, moving away from the camera into the dark.

In the foreground, one box has been lifted off the line and placed on a workbench beside it: the same size, but finished in glossy lemon yellow (exact colour #FFD60A), with crisp hand-folded edges. A designer's tools sit next to it: a pencil, a metal ruler, a cutting mat.

Lighting: a single warm work lamp lights the yellow box and the bench. The conveyor line is cool and in shadow. Real dust in the air catching the light.

Camera: 35mm lens, f/2, low angle at bench height, shallow depth of field, the line of grey boxes blurred in the background. Natural film grain.

Colours: near-black #0E0E10, charcoal #17171D, cool greys, and the one yellow #FFD60A.

Format: landscape 16:10, 2000 x 1250 px, with empty dark space on the left for a headline.

Do NOT add: text, logos, watermarks, people, robots, glowing screens, purple or blue neon, or a 3D render look.
```

## Prompt 4: light version for the light page (#F2F2EE)

Use this if you want the photo to sit on the light hero without a dark block.

```
A realistic, minimal studio photograph on a seamless warm off-white background (exact colour #F2F2EE). Rows of identical light grey ceramic coffee mugs, all the same shape, all matte pale grey (#D9D9DF), arranged in a tight, perfect grid, shot from a 45-degree angle.

One mug in the third row is different: a deep near-black (#0E0E10) mug with a thick hand-painted lemon-yellow (#FFD60A) brush stroke around it, clearly made by hand, slightly imperfect.

Lighting: big soft window light from the left, gentle natural shadows on the white background, real ceramic texture and small imperfections.

Camera: 85mm lens, f/4, the front and back rows softly out of focus. Subtle film grain.

Colours: only #F2F2EE, pale greys, #0E0E10 and #FFD60A.

Format: portrait 4:5, 1600 x 2000 px.

Do NOT add: text, logos, watermarks, people, glow, purple, gradients, or a CGI look. It must look like a real photo.
```
