# Hero visual v3: more creative ideas

The yellow chair works, but "one different object in a crowd" is a common stock-photo idea. These concepts tell **our** story more directly: a brand that came from one prompt is **mass-produced**, and a MuzMesh launch is **made by hand by people**. Each idea shows a machine making copies next to something a person crafted.

All prompts keep the same rules: real photography, MuzMesh yellow #FFD60A as the only bright colour, near-black #0E0E10 and warm greys, no text in the image.

**Paste this at the end of any prompt if it starts looking like AI:**
> Make it look like a real photo shot on a professional camera: natural imperfections, dust, scuffs, uneven light, real textures, subtle film grain. No CGI shine, no glow, no perfect symmetry. Exact yellow #FFD60A, not orange or gold. No text, logos or watermarks.

---

## Idea 1 (my pick): The rubber stamp
**Story:** one prompt = one stamp, pressed a hundred times. Ours is drawn by hand.
**Why it works:** very easy to read in one second, and it is literally "same logo, everywhere".

```
A realistic overhead studio photograph of a large sheet of warm off-white paper (#F2F2EE) on a dark wooden desk. The paper is covered edge to edge in rows of the same generic logo, pressed with a worn black rubber stamp: the same blob shape over and over, slightly uneven ink, some faded, some smudged. The used rubber stamp lies on its side at the top right, next to a black ink pad.

In the middle of the sheet, one spot breaks the pattern: a bold, confident, hand-drawn mark in thick lemon-yellow marker ink (exact colour #FFD60A), clearly drawn by a person, with visible marker strokes and a sharp black pencil outline around it. A black pencil, an eraser and a steel ruler lie next to it.

Lighting: soft daylight from the top left, real paper texture, gentle shadows from the tools.
Camera: 50mm lens, shot straight down, f/5.6, subtle film grain.
Colours: #F2F2EE paper, black ink #0E0E10, warm greys, one yellow #FFD60A.
Format: portrait 4:5, 1600 x 2000 px.
Do NOT add: readable text, real brand logos, hands, people, glow, purple, CGI look.
```

## Idea 2: The photocopier
**Story:** most brands are a copy of a copy. Ours is the original.
**Why it works:** "copy" is exactly the villain. Strong, a bit funny, memorable.

```
A realistic, moody photograph of an old office photocopier in a dark room, its lid half open, a soft green-white scan light glowing faintly inside. A huge, messy stack of identical printed pages spills out of the output tray and onto the floor, hundreds of the same grey generic web page, each copy slightly more faded than the last.

On top of the photocopier, placed carefully apart from the mess, sits one single original: a crisp, heavy, hand-made poster with a bold black and lemon-yellow (#FFD60A) design, held flat by a steel ruler, with a pencil beside it.

Lighting: one warm spotlight from above on the original poster; the copies fall into cool shadow.
Camera: 35mm lens, f/2.8, eye level, shallow depth of field, film grain.
Colours: near-black #0E0E10, charcoal #17171D, cold greys, one yellow #FFD60A.
Format: portrait 4:5, 1600 x 2000 px, dark empty space at the top.
Do NOT add: readable text, logos, people, neon, purple, CGI look.
```

## Idea 3: Off the rack vs tailored
**Story:** a one-prompt brand is off the rack. Ours is tailored to fit you.
**Why it works:** everyone understands "tailored". It feels premium and human.

```
A realistic photograph inside a dim, minimal tailor's workshop. In the background, a long clothing rail holds dozens of identical cheap grey jackets on identical wire hangers, all the same size, all slightly creased, fading into shadow.

In the foreground, on a dressmaker's mannequin, stands one beautifully tailored jacket in lemon yellow (exact colour #FFD60A), with white chalk marks on the fabric, pins in the shoulder and a tape measure draped around the neck. A pair of black tailor's scissors and a pin cushion sit on the wooden work table beside it.

Lighting: a single warm lamp on the yellow jacket, soft window light from the side, the rail of grey jackets in cool shadow.
Camera: 50mm lens, f/2, shallow depth of field, film grain, real fabric texture and loose threads.
Colours: near-black #0E0E10, charcoal greys, warm wood, one yellow #FFD60A.
Format: portrait 4:5, 1600 x 2000 px.
Do NOT add: people, faces, text, logos, glow, purple, CGI look.
```

## Idea 4: The vending machine
**Story:** "a brand in one button press". Every slot is the same product.
**Why it works:** sharp, modern, and points straight at the AI prompt habit.

```
A realistic night-time photograph of a single vending machine standing alone against a dark concrete wall. Behind the glass, every slot holds the exact same plain grey box with the same generic blob logo, row after row, all identical. The machine's cold white light is the only light in the scene.

On the concrete floor in front of the machine stands one hand-made box, slightly bigger, wrapped in heavy lemon-yellow paper (exact colour #FFD60A), tied with black string, with neat hand-folded corners and a visible pencil mark. It is lit by a warm side light that the machine does not have.

Camera: 35mm lens, f/2.8, low angle, film grain, real dust and scratches on the glass.
Colours: near-black #0E0E10, cold greys, one yellow #FFD60A.
Format: portrait 4:5, 1600 x 2000 px.
Do NOT add: readable text, price labels, real brands, people, neon colours, purple, CGI look.
```

## Idea 5: Cast vs carved
**Story:** the mould makes copies. A person carves the original.
**Why it works:** shows craft and time, which is what "designed by people" means.

```
A realistic close-up studio photograph on a dark workbench. On the left, a grey silicone mould has produced a neat tray of identical small grey plaster shapes, all exactly the same, all with the same tiny air bubbles.

On the right, one piece is being carved by hand from a block of lemon-yellow material (exact colour #FFD60A): a bold, unique shape with visible chisel marks and fresh shavings around it. A wooden-handled carving chisel lies next to it, and a small brass ruler.

Lighting: soft warm light from the right on the yellow piece, cooler and flatter light on the grey casts.
Camera: 90mm macro lens, f/4, shallow depth of field, film grain, real dust and shavings.
Colours: near-black #0E0E10, greys, warm wood, one yellow #FFD60A.
Format: landscape 3:2, 2100 x 1400 px, empty dark space on the left for a headline.
Do NOT add: hands, people, text, logos, glow, purple, CGI look.
```

## Idea 6: The chair, pushed further
**Story:** same as your favourite, but now the yellow chair is clearly *made by people*.
**Why it works:** keeps what you liked, adds our "hand-designed" message.

```
A realistic editorial photograph of a dark hall filled with rows of identical matte black plastic chairs on a worn concrete floor, fading into darkness.

In the middle, one chair is lemon yellow (exact colour #FFD60A), and it is clearly still in the designer's workshop: strips of black masking tape on the seat, faint pencil measurement lines, a small paper template taped to the back, and a steel ruler and pencil resting on the seat. A single overhead spotlight falls on it.

Camera: 50mm lens, f/2.8, slightly above eye level, shallow depth of field, film grain, real scuffs and dust.
Colours: near-black #0E0E10, charcoal, concrete grey, one yellow #FFD60A.
Format: portrait 4:5, 1600 x 2000 px, dark empty space at the top.
Do NOT add: readable text, people, glow, purple, CGI look.
```
