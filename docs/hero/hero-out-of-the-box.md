# Hero visual v5: out-of-the-box ideas

New rule for this round: **no "one yellow thing in a grid".** Each idea uses a scene from everyday life where *sameness is a real problem*, so people get the point in one second and smile.

Every prompt: realistic photo, matte materials, MuzMesh yellow #FFD60A as the only bright colour, near-black #0E0E10 and warm greys. Paste this at the end of any prompt if it looks too AI:
> Real photo from a professional camera: natural imperfections, real textures, uneven light, subtle film grain. No CGI shine, no glow, no purple or blue. Exact yellow #FFD60A, not orange. No logos or watermarks.

---

## 1. The baggage carousel (my pick)
**Why it's different:** everyone has stood at the airport waiting for a black suitcase that looks like everyone else's. A brand that looks like everyone else's gets lost the same way. The yellow case is the one you spot from across the hall.
**Headline:** *Easy to spot. Hard to forget.*

```
A realistic, cinematic photograph of an airport baggage carousel, shot from a low angle at the end of the belt. Dozens of identical matte black hard-shell suitcases ride the belt in a long curve, all the same model, same size, same colour (#0E0E10), each with the same plain grey tag, fading into the soft-focus background.

Coming around the bend toward the camera: one suitcase that is clearly different. Matte lemon yellow (exact colour #FFD60A), hand-painted, with visible brush strokes, a few black hand-drawn stripes, worn leather handle and travel scuffs. It is sharp and in focus; everything else is softly blurred.

Lighting: cool, flat airport overhead light on the black cases; a warm shaft of window light catches the yellow one.
Camera: 35mm lens, f/2, low angle at belt height, shallow depth of field, film grain.
Format: landscape 16:10, 2000 x 1250 px, calm empty space in the upper left for a headline.
Colours: near-black, concrete and steel greys, one yellow #FFD60A.
Do NOT add: people, readable text, airline logos, glow, purple, CGI look.
```

## 2. The cookie cutter shaped like a prompt bar
**Why it's different:** "cookie-cutter brand" is a real saying. We make it literal: the cutter is shaped like an AI chat box with a send arrow. It's the villain as a physical object, and it's a little bit funny.
**Headline:** *Your brand isn't a cookie-cutter.*

```
A realistic overhead food photograph on a dark slate surface (#0E0E10) dusted with flour. A sheet of rolled-out pale dough has been cut over and over with the same steel cookie cutter: the cutter is shaped like a long rounded chat input box with a small arrow button at the right end, like an AI prompt bar. Dozens of identical pale cookies in that exact shape are lined up on a baking tray, all the same.

The steel cutter itself lies at an angle on the floury surface, slightly dented from use.

In the bottom right, apart from the rest, sits one cookie shaped completely by hand: a bold, unusual, confident shape, iced in thick lemon-yellow royal icing (#FFD60A) with clean hand-piped black lines. A piping bag rests next to it.

Lighting: soft daylight from the top left, real flour texture, crumbs, gentle shadows.
Camera: shot straight down, 50mm lens, f/5.6, film grain.
Format: portrait 4:5, 1600 x 2000 px.
Do NOT add: readable text, hands, logos, glow, purple, CGI look.
```

## 3. The gallery
**Why it's different:** it shows the *result* we promise: people stop and remember you. A gallery wall of identical "AI art" blobs that everyone walks past, and one small hand-made piece with a crowd in front of it.
**Headline:** *People walk past the same. They stop for the different.*

```
A realistic, wide photograph inside a minimal modern art gallery with warm grey concrete floors and white walls. A long wall holds twelve identical framed prints, all the same soft grey abstract gradient blob, evenly spaced, perfectly bland. A few visitors, seen from behind and slightly blurred in motion, walk past them without looking.

Near the end of the wall hangs one smaller frame that is different: a bold, hand-painted composition in black ink and lemon yellow (#FFD60A), with visible brush texture. A small group of five people stands still in front of it, leaning in, one pointing.

Lighting: soft gallery spotlights, the yellow piece lit slightly warmer.
Camera: 24mm lens, f/4, eye level, slight motion blur on the walking visitors, film grain.
Colours: white, warm greys, black clothing, one yellow #FFD60A. Visitors wear black, grey or white only.
Format: landscape 16:9, 2000 x 1125 px.
Do NOT add: readable text, faces looking at camera, logos, glow, purple, CGI look.
```

## 4. The parrots
**Why it's different:** our one-liner says AI startups *look and sound* the same. Parrots repeat what they hear, just like copy from a prompt. It's a playful, very memorable image.
**Headline:** *Don't sound like everyone else.*

```
A realistic wildlife-style photograph of a long black power line against a plain pale grey sky (#D9D9DF). Perched along it in a perfect row: twenty identical grey parrots, all facing the same way, all with their beaks open at exactly the same moment, as if repeating the same word.

In the middle of the row, one bird is different: a bright lemon-yellow canary (#FFD60A), turned the other way, singing on its own, beak open, feathers slightly ruffled.

Lighting: soft overcast daylight, real feather detail.
Camera: 400mm telephoto, f/5.6, compressed perspective, the line and birds sharp, film grain.
Format: landscape 16:9, 2000 x 1125 px, plenty of empty sky above for a headline.
Colours: greys, black wire, one yellow #FFD60A.
Do NOT add: text, logos, glow, extra colours, cartoon look.
```

## 5. Pop-art shelf (a nod to Warhol's soup cans)
**Why it's different:** an art-history joke about mass production. A supermarket shelf of identical cans labelled with the same bland brand, and one can whose label has been painted by hand.
**Headline:** *Mass-produced brands look mass-produced.*

```
A realistic, straight-on photograph of a supermarket shelf, tightly cropped, holding four rows of identical tin cans. Every can has the same plain grey paper label with the same simple grey blob logo and no words. Perfectly lined up, cold fluorescent light.

In the third row, one can has been taken out, turned slightly, and put back: its label has been painted over by hand in thick black and lemon-yellow (#FFD60A) paint, with visible brush strokes and a little drip running down the side.

Camera: 50mm lens, f/4, straight on, slight fluorescent green cast removed, film grain.
Format: portrait 4:5, 1600 x 2000 px.
Colours: greys, steel, black, one yellow #FFD60A.
Do NOT add: readable words, price tags, real brands, people, glow, purple, CGI look.
```

## 6. The fortune cookies (funniest)
**Why it's different:** it makes fun of the villain. Every cookie holds the same tired AI-startup line. One holds a hand-written note. People will screenshot this.
**Headline:** *Everyone got the same fortune.*

```
A realistic close-up food photograph on a matte black table (#0E0E10). A pile of cracked-open fortune cookies, and every paper slip pulled out of them is printed with exactly the same small line of grey type: "Revolutionize your workflow with AI." Dozens of identical slips lie scattered and overlapping.

In the foreground, one slip is different: a slightly bigger piece of lemon-yellow paper (#FFD60A), with a short line hand-written in thick black marker: "Be remembered." A black marker lies next to it, cap off.

Lighting: soft warm side light, real paper and cookie texture, crumbs.
Camera: 85mm lens, f/2.8, shallow depth of field, the foreground slip sharp, film grain.
Format: square 1600 x 1600 px.
Spell the text exactly: "Revolutionize your workflow with AI." and "Be remembered."
Do NOT add: other text, logos, glow, purple, CGI look.
```

## Ranking
1. **Baggage carousel.** Everyone has lived it, it's warm and human, and it maps straight to "stand out and be remembered".
2. **Cookie-cutter prompt bar.** It turns the villain into an object, and the pun lands instantly.
3. **Fortune cookies.** The funniest and most shareable. Great for social posts and a strong second hero.
4. **Gallery.** The most premium, and it shows the *result*.
5. **Parrots.** A memorable "sounds the same" angle.
6. **Pop-art shelf.** A smart nod to art history, though less obvious to everyone.
