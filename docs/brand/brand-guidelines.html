01 · The idea

Look launched, not generated.

MuzMesh is the design partner for AI startups. Brand, landing page and launch kit, designed by people, ready in five days. Our own brand has to prove the point: if it looks like a prompt, nobody believes the pitch.

One accentBlack, bone and one orange.

Orange is a signal, not a mood. It marks the one thing to press, the bullet on a label, a live dot. Everything else is black and off-white.

Show the workReal artifacts beat effects.

We show build logs, browser windows, tickets and finished pages. No glowing orbs, no gradients on text, no sparkle icon.

Launch languageA launch, told like one.

Mono labels, a five-day countdown, a launch pass with IDEA → LIVE. The vocabulary of shipping, set calmly.

One typefaceInter Tight for everything.

Character comes from weight, spacing and the two-tone headline, never from a second display font. Mono is for data only.

Personality: considered, plain-spoken, quietly confident, founder-to-founder. We sound like the designer you’d hire, not the agency pitch deck.

Do

  • Use the SVG files. Never retype the wordmark in a design tool.
  • Bone (#F4F4F1) on black, ink on paper or orange. Nothing else.
  • Let the giant cropped wordmark close a page (the footer), in stone grey.
  • In the nav, the 22px live-text version is fine: same font, same tracking.

Don’t

  • Colour the wordmark orange, or put a gradient, glow or outline on it.
  • Add a sparkle, spark or any “AI” symbol next to it.
  • Capitalise it in the logo. In running text, write MuzMesh.
  • Stretch, rotate or re-track it, or set it smaller than 72px wide.

Status: this wordmark is the working logo until a custom-drawn mark is commissioned. The files are named so a future logo can replace them without touching any page.

2 · Four weights, four jobs

400Regular

All running text: body, leads, captions, nav links, table cells.

500Medium

Headlines (display, H2, H3), big numbers and prices, and bold words inside a headline or lead.

600Semibold

Every button, H4 card titles, and bold words inside body text. Nothing else.

700Bold

The wordmark only. Never a headline, never a number, never a button.

3 · Numbers line up

4 · Kerning and OpenType

Kerning, standard ligatures and contextual alternates are on for every piece of text, set once on .mm. Never turn kerning off, and never fix a pair by hand with a span. If a pair looks wrong, the wrong font is loading.

Fixed sizes come from one short list. Anything between two steps is a mistake, not a choice. Headline sizes are fluid (clamp()) and come only from the tokens.

7 · Punctuation

"It's ready"“It’s ready”

Curly quotes and apostrophes.

3-5 days3–5 days

En dash for ranges, no spaces.

5 days ↵ 5 days

Non-breaking space between a number and its unit (&nbsp;).

Wait...Wait…

One ellipsis character. Rare.

2x faster2× faster

Multiplication sign, not the letter x.

Our ProcessOur process

Sentence case. Only mono labels are uppercase.

Do

  • Take size, weight, line height and tracking together from one token. Never mix a size from one style with tracking from another.
  • Split a headline into setup (dim) and payoff (full colour).
  • Mono labels in uppercase with the 5px orange square in front.
  • Tabular figures on every price, countdown and table.

Don’t

  • Half-pixel sans sizes (12.5, 13.5, 14.5px) or one-off sizes such as 18 or 19px.
  • Spaced-out Inter Tight caps as an eyebrow. Eyebrows are mono.
  • Bold (700) headlines or numbers, or a 500 button next to a 600 one.
  • Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Serif or Newsreader for MuzMesh. They appear on the homepage only inside the example client brands.

Write your own

[Subject: who or what, doing what, where] + the brand style + Aspect ratio [4:5 · 1:1 · 16:9 · 9:16] + “Avoid: …”. Describe a real scene a photographer could shoot. Name the light, not the mood: “desk lamp and window light”, not “inspiring”.

Any toolGenerate four, keep one. Upscale before grading. Never ask for words inside the image.

After you generate

  • For black layouts, convert to the duotone grade: black #0A0A0C to bone #F4F4F1 (Photoshop Gradient Map, Figma “Luminosity” blend over a black fill, or any duotone tool).
  • For case studies and the founder’s own photos, keep the natural grade: saturation down to about 72%, no filters.
  • Check hands, faces and edges at 100%. Reject anything with warped fingers, fake lettering or plastic skin.
  • Crop so the subject sits on one third and the dark space holds the headline. Text over a photo always sits on the dark gradient.

Where generated images can’t go

  • Generated images are for mood, backgrounds, textures and editorial scenes.
  • Never present a generated person as a real client, founder, team member or testimonial, and never use generated images in case studies. Those use real photos and real screenshots.
  • Never generate our logo, a client’s logo or UI. Add type and screens afterwards in the layout.

11 · Voice and copy

Say it plainly. Then stop.

We write like a founder talking to a founder: short sentences, real numbers, no hype. The reader should finish a section knowing exactly what they get and what it costs.

HeadlinesSetup, then payoff.

“A website is one page of your launch. We design the rest.” “One price. No surprises.”

ButtonsSay what happens.

“Plan my launch”, “See what’s included”, “Build my idea”. First person on the main CTA. Never “Get started” or “Learn more”.

NumbersExact and true.

“$500 to set up, $200 a month.” “Live in five business days.” No invented stats, testimonials, client logos or “10x”.

Words we uselaunch · kit · pass · founder · live · yours

The human line is always “designed by people”. Words we avoid: revolutionary, unlock, seamless, supercharge, cutting-edge, AI-powered, game-changer, and emoji.

Write

  • A designer draws your logo, sets your brand and designs your page in Figma.
  • We shape and edit your words, but we don’t write them from scratch.
  • Need more pages? We’ll quote it before we start, never after.

Not

  • Unlock your startup’s full potential with our AI-powered branding.
  • We craft pixel-perfect, world-class experiences ✨
  • 90% of startups fail because of bad design. (unsupported)

Names and spelling: MuzMesh in text, muzmesh in the logo, MuzMesh Creative for the legal name and footer. Five days in copy, “5 days” in labels and the pass. Curly quotes and apostrophes. Sentence case everywhere except mono labels.

16 · Audit and open items

What we checked, what we changed.

The guideline was audited against the homepage stylesheet (web/src/styles/home.css) and the rest of the site. These are the gaps found, how they were settled, and what still needs a decision.

FixedTwo hover colours for the orange button. The nav pill went darker (#CC3D08, 3.9:1 with dark text, fails) while the big CTAs went lighter (#FF6A20). Hover is now always lighter.
FixedFaint text below AA on panels. 50% bone measured 4.5:1 on raised panels. Faint is now 56% (6.0:1).
FixedTicket small print at 4.0:1. Paper labels now use #595857 (6.2:1) instead of 58% ink.
FixedOrange text on light pages failed. Added signal-ink #B33A06 (5.2:1 on paper, 6.0:1 under white text). The older site pages now use it in place of the indigo placeholder.
FixedTwo eyebrow styles. Some sections used spaced Inter Tight caps, others mono. The rule is now mono for every label; the quiet variant drops the square.
FixedOne-off colours. Footer grey, the dim headline grey and the form error colours now have names (stone, ash, danger) instead of loose hex values.
FixedDots vs squares. Rule written down: a square marks a label or list item, a circle marks something live or a position.
FixedRetired v1. Yellow #FFD60A, Archivo and the paper-first layout are archived. The four hero test palettes (Oxblood, Teal, Magenta, Green) were explorations for an earlier hero and are not part of the brand.
AddedIconography and imagery. v2 first shipped without them. There is now a 28-icon set on one grid, a photo grade (natural and duotone), texture rules and visual do/don’t examples.
AddedSocial kit. Profiles, banner safe zones, feed rhythm, a carousel structure, story safe zones, a caption template and a size table for every platform we use.
AddedAI ready. The rules now ship as BRAND.md, a short system prompt, a starter page and a checker, generated from the same tokens, so any AI tool produces on-brand work.
AddedImage prompt library. One brand style block, an avoid list and seven ready prompts with Midjourney versions, plus where generated images may and may not be used. The old “never generated” rule now reads: never as proof of real people or work.
FixedTypography drift. The homepage used 70 font sizes, 25 letter-spacing values and three weights for buttons. The system now has one tracking value per size, four weights with one job each, eyebrows in mono only, tabular figures on numbers, and a checker that flags off-scale sizes, tracking and weights.
