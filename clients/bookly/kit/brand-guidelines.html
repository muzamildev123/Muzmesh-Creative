01 · The idea

Bookly in one breath.

Bookly turns the books you mean to read into ten-minute conversations you actually finish. The brand should feel like a good library: calm, warm, a little bookish, and never in a hurry.

01Calm over clever

Lots of space, soft paper tones and one deep green. Nothing blinks, nothing shouts.

02Books first

Real covers, real quotes, real page numbers. The product is about reading, so the brand looks like reading.

03One serif, one sans

Fraunces for headlines gives the bookish warmth. Inter does all the work everywhere else.

04Plain words

We explain like a well-read friend, not a productivity guru.

Personality: Warm, clear, quietly clever, never smug.

05 · Iconography

Thin lines, round ends.

28 icons on a 16px grid with a 1.6px stroke, drawn in the current text colour. Use them at 16, 20 or 24px. Accent-coloured only when they mark the one action.

06 · Imagery

Real books, real light.

Real books, real hands, real reading corners. Warm daylight, linen and wood. Never stock 'success' photos or glowing brains.

Do

  • Books on tables, in hands, on trains.
  • Warm natural light, muted colour.
  • Close crops of pages and margins.

Don’t

  • AI-generated people or robots.
  • Neon, gradients, glowing brains.
  • Stacks of money, trophies, mountain summits.

Generating a photo? Prompt for: “documentary photo, warm window light, a person reading a paperback in a quiet corner, linen and wood tones, muted colour, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos”. Never ask an image model for text or logos.

09 · Voice and copy

Like a friend who reads a lot.

We sound like a well-read friend: warm, specific, a little playful, never preachy about productivity.

Words we useread · finish · remember · chapter · your shelf
Words we avoid

hack, 10x, grind, unlock, revolutionary, AI-powered, game-changer, seamless, and emoji in headlines.

ButtonsSay what happens.

“Start reading”, “See a sample”. Never “Click here” or “Learn more”.

Names

Bookly in text, bookly (lowercase) in the logo, Bookly Inc. for legal.

Write

  • “Finish the book you started in March.”
  • “Ten minutes a day. One chapter at a time.”
  • “We read the boring bits so you don't have to.”

Not

  • “Unlock 10x knowledge with AI-powered reading hacks 🚀”
  • “The ultimate productivity tool for high performers.”
  • “Read 100 books a year!”

12 · Use with AI

Any AI, still on brand.

This kit includes an AI pack: the whole brand as plain text plus a short prompt. Give it to ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini or a coding tool and what comes back uses Bookly’s colours, fonts, components and voice.

ChatGPTProject or custom GPT

Upload ai/BRAND.md, ai/starter.html and the brand folder. Paste the prompt below into the instructions.

ClaudeProject

Add the same files to project knowledge and paste the prompt into the project instructions.

DeepSeek, Gemini, othersAttach and paste

Attach ai/BRAND.md and paste the prompt as your first message. In Gemini, save it as a Gem.

Cursor, Copilot, CodexDrop the folder in

AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md and the rules files make coding tools read BRAND.md on their own.

ai/system-prompt.txt
You design and write for Bookly. Follow the attached BRAND.md exactly; it wins over any other style.
Essentials: light theme by default, background #FAF6EE, text #1E2420; dark theme #14231C with #F3EFE6 text. One accent, #1F7A55, only for the single primary button per view, links, label markers and focus, with #FFFFFF text on it. Accent text uses #1F7A55 on #FAF6EE.
Fraunces for headlines, Inter for everything else. No other fonts or colours. Every section opens with an UPPERCASE label.
Voice: We sound like a well-read friend: warm, specific, a little playful, never preachy about productivity. Never say: hack, 10x, grind, unlock, revolutionary, AI-powered, game-changer, seamless.
No emoji in headlines, no AI-generated people, no invented numbers. Before answering, run the checklist in BRAND.md section 12.

Check the result: run node ai/check-brand.mjs page.html. It lists any colour, font, emoji or banned word that is off-brand, line by line.
